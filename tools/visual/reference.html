Star System Preview

Prototype: a K-type star with two hypothetical planets, driven by example numbers of the kind Shaula, Fang and the catalogues would supply. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.

Hypothetical · artist's impression
WebGL is not available in this browser, so the 3D view cannot start.

Everything here is computed in the page from the example inputs shown: Kepler's third law for orbit size and speed, insolation and equilibrium temperature (Bond albedo 0.3) from the star, and colour from a blackbody at the star's temperature. Distances are compressed unless true scale is on; at true scale the planets are almost invisible, which is realistic. Fang scores are placeholders.
